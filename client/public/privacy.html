1. Who we are

Mavino is a personal productivity platform for students. The hosted service at mavino.net is operated by Jakub Horák (the "Controller", "we", "us"), acting as the data controller for the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the Czech Act on the Processing of Personal Data (Act No. 110/2019 Coll., "ZOOÚ").

Mavino is open-source software licensed under the AGPL-3.0. This policy covers only the instance we operate. The software itself processes no data without an operator running it.

2. What data we collect and why

Mavino is a data-rich productivity tool. The data we process falls into the categories below.

2.1 Account & authentication data

  • Username, display name, email (optional) — to identify you and send password-reset emails.
  • Password — stored only as a bcrypt hash (salt rounds 10). We never store or see your plaintext password.
  • Two-factor (TOTP) secret — encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and used only to verify your 2FA codes.
  • Refresh tokens & device labels — SHA-256 hashes of refresh tokens plus a device label derived from your browser's User-Agent, so you can review and revoke active sessions.

2.2 Academic data

  • Grades & assignments — course names, codes, scores, weights, and categories (synced from VUT or entered manually).
  • Timetable — class times and locations (synced from VUT or entered manually).
  • Calendar events — titles, descriptions, times, and locations from manual entry, tasks, VUT, assignments, ICS imports, Moodle, or Microsoft Graph.

2.3 Notes, tasks & study content

  • Notes — full markdown content (including LaTeX), titles, tags, and folder structure.
  • Tasks — titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, statuses, and recurrence rules.
  • Flashcards — front/back content and spaced-repetition review history (SM-2 algorithm state).
  • Study Hub data — chat conversations with the AI assistant, source texts extracted from your notes/files/pastes/Moodle/web URLs, study sessions, highlights, and teacher-mode sessions.
  • AI memories — facts and preferences the assistant remembers about you (you can view and delete these in the app).

2.4 Files & media

  • Uploaded files — documents, images, PDFs, and other files you upload, stored on the server's disk.
  • Voice recordings — audio you record in the Voice app (webm/ogg/mp4/wav) is persisted on the server and linked to a transcribed note. See Section 4.3 for where audio is sent for transcription.
  • Lecture videos — video files you submit for the lecture-to-notes pipeline are processed and then the temporary working files are deleted.
  • Moodle materials — virtual file references (not the file bytes) for materials synced from Moodle; the bytes are streamed on-demand from Moodle.

2.5 Third-party credentials you provide

To enable integrations, you may store credentials that Mavino uses on your behalf. All are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM:

  • VUT username & password — for VUT SSO login, grades/timetable sync, and Moodle ride-along authentication.
  • LLM provider API key (and optional fallback) — for the AI assistant. Provider may be OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, Google, xAI, Meta, Cerebras, or a local Ollama instance.
  • ElevenLabs API key — for text-to-speech.
  • Microsoft Graph OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret, refresh token, tenant ID) — for Outlook calendar sync.
  • Spotify OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret, refresh token) — for music playback control.
  • Mapy.cz API key — for maps and route planning.
  • Ntfy server URL & token — for push notifications.

2.6 Other personal data

  • Habits & habit logs — habit names and daily completion values.
  • Trips & hiking tours — planned routes, waypoints, POIs, and AI-narrated summaries.
  • Whiteboards, workspaces, reminders, focus sessions — your productivity content and layouts.
  • Settings & preferences — theme, accent, notification preferences, AI instructions, etc.
  • Anonymous usage statistics — aggregate per-feature daily hit counts with no user ID attached, used only for the admin dashboard.

4. Where your data is sent (third parties)

Mavino integrates with several third-party services. Below is a complete list of where your data is transmitted, what is sent, and why. You control most of these integrations — they only activate when you connect them in Settings → Integrations or Settings → Mavino Assistant.

4.1 LLM providers (AI assistant, Study Hub, lecture notes, voice cleanup)

When you use the Mavino assistant, Study Hub, lecture-to-notes, or voice-note cleanup, your data is sent to the LLM provider you configured (or a server-wide fallback). Providers include OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, Google, xAI, Meta, Cerebras, or a self-hosted Ollama instance.

What is sent:

  • Your chat conversation history (messages, including any files/notes you attach).
  • Source content for Study Hub — extracted text from your notes, uploaded files, pasted text, Moodle resources, and fetched web pages.
  • Context about your calendar, tasks, habits, due flashcards, and AI memories (so the assistant can give relevant answers).
  • Your custom AI instructions.
  • Raw voice transcripts (for the cleanup pass) and lecture video transcripts / slide OCR text.

Purpose: to generate AI responses, summaries, flashcards, podcasts, quizzes, and cleaned transcripts.

Important: Each LLM provider has its own privacy policy and may retain or train on your inputs depending on their terms and your account type. Check your provider's policy. If you use a self-hosted Ollama instance, your data does not leave that instance.

4.2 ElevenLabs (text-to-speech)

When you use Teach Me mode or play a generated podcast, the text to be spoken is sent to ElevenLabs' API (api.elevenlabs.io) along with your configured voice ID and model ID. A fallback to Microsoft Edge TTS (no data leaves the server) is available.

4.3 Voice & lecture transcription (Whisper-compatible)

When you record a voice note or submit a lecture video, the audio file bytes are sent to an OpenAI-compatible Whisper transcription endpoint (configured by the operator or your LLM credentials). The audio is transmitted for transcription; the resulting text is stored as a note. The audio file itself is also stored on the Mavino server (see Section 5).

4.4 Microsoft Graph (Outlook calendar)

If you connect Microsoft Calendar, Mavino uses your OAuth credentials to read and write calendar events (subjects, bodies, times, locations) via the Microsoft Graph API (graph.microsoft.com). Your refresh token is exchanged at Microsoft's token endpoint.

4.5 VUT SSO & Moodle (Brno University of Technology)

If you connect the VUT integration, Mavino logs in to VUT (vut.cz / id.vut.cz) using your stored VUT username and password, then fetches your grades, timetable, and updates. The VUT session is reused to authenticate to Moodle (moodle.vut.cz) via OIDC ride-along — no separate Moodle credentials are stored. Session cookies are held in memory only (25-minute TTL) and are never persisted to disk.

4.6 Mapy.cz (maps & route planning)

If you use the Maps app, your geocoding queries, routing requests (start/end/waypoints), and POI searches are sent to the mapy.com REST API (api.mapy.com) using your stored API key. Tile images are loaded directly by your browser with the API key as a query parameter.

4.7 Spotify (music playback)

If you connect Spotify, Mavino uses your OAuth credentials to control playback and fetch track metadata via the Spotify Web API. Track names/artists are also sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to fetch synced lyrics.

4.8 Web search (Tavily / Brave / DuckDuckGo)

When the AI assistant or Study Hub performs a web search, your search query (and an optional region code) is sent to Tavily, Brave, or DuckDuckGo. No other personal data is included.

4.9 URL fetching & in-app browser

When you ask the assistant to read a web page or use the in-app Browser app, Mavino fetches the URL you provide. The request includes a User-Agent header and, for the Browser app, a per-user in-memory cookie jar (24-hour TTL, not persisted). Server-side SSRF protection blocks private network ranges.

4.10 Ntfy (push notifications)

If you configure Ntfy, notification messages (title, body, tags, priority) are sent to your configured Ntfy server (default ntfy.sh) along with an optional bearer token.

4.11 SMTP (transactional email)

When you request a password reset, an email containing a reset link is sent to your email address via the operator's configured SMTP server. No marketing emails are sent.

5. How and where data is stored

  • Database: PostgreSQL 16, running on the hosted server (EU / Czech Republic VPS). All structured data (account, notes, tasks, grades, calendar, chat history, etc.) is stored here.
  • File storage: Uploaded files and voice recordings are stored on the server's disk (Docker volume).
  • Cache / rate limiting: Redis 7 is used for rate-limiting and ephemeral caching. No personal data is persisted in Redis beyond short-lived session cookies (VUT/Moodle: 25 min; in-app browser: 24 h).
  • Backups: Daily encrypted backups are retained for 14 days (see Section 6).
  • Encryption at rest: All third-party credentials and TOTP secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed (salt rounds 10).
  • In transit: All traffic to and from mavino.net is encrypted with TLS (terminated by nginx / Let's Encrypt).

6. How long we keep your data

  • Account & content: We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 9), all your data is permanently removed via cascade delete.
  • Voice recordings & uploaded files: Retained until you delete them or delete your account. Lecture-pipeline temporary working files are deleted after processing completes.
  • Backups: Daily backups are retained for 14 days, then automatically deleted. After you delete your account, your data may persist in a backup for up to 14 days before being permanently gone.
  • Refresh tokens: Rotated on each use and expire automatically; revocable per-device at any time.
  • Password-reset tokens: Expire after 1 hour.
  • Session cookies (VUT/Moodle/browser): In-memory only, 25 min (VUT/Moodle) or 24 h (browser) TTL, never persisted.
  • Anonymous usage stats: Aggregate daily counts, no user ID, retained indefinitely.

7. Security measures

  • Encryption at rest for all credentials and TOTP secrets (AES-256-GCM).
  • Bcrypt password hashing (salt rounds 10) — plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • TLS for all network traffic.
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived access tokens and rotated, revocable refresh tokens.
  • Optional two-factor authentication (TOTP) for your account.
  • SSRF protection on URL-fetching features to prevent access to private networks.
  • Redis-based rate limiting to prevent abuse.
  • Per-user data isolation — every database query is scoped to the authenticated user; there is no cross-user access.
  • Encrypted daily backups with 14-day retention.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and you within 72 hours, as required by GDPR Art. 33–34.

8. Your GDPR rights

Under the GDPR and Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — request a copy of your data via Settings → Data & Storage → Export or by emailing us.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — correct inaccurate data in-app or by emailing us.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17) — delete your account as described in Section 9.
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — export your data in JSON format via Settings → Data & Storage → Export.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interest (e.g. anonymous analytics).
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — disconnect any integration in Settings → Integrations at any time; the associated credentials are deleted.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) — with the Czech Data Protection Authority (ÚOOÚ) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email tevuori@mavino.net. We respond within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, per Art. 12(3)).

9. How to delete your account

Account deletion is self-service, immediate, and irreversible.
  1. Open Settings → Data & Storage.
  2. In the Danger zone card, enter your password.
  3. Click Delete account and confirm the two warning dialogs.

This calls DELETE /auth/account with your password. On confirmation, the server permanently deletes your user record and cascades the deletion to all associated data: notes, tasks, courses, grades, calendar events, files, voice recordings, flashcards, chat history, AI memories, study sessions, habits, trips, whiteboards, reminders, focus sessions, and all stored third-party credentials. The deletion is final and cannot be undone.

The only trace that may remain is in the daily backups, which are retained for up to 14 days and then automatically overwritten. If you need accelerated removal from backups, email tevuori@mavino.net.

Prefer to export first? Use Settings → Data & Storage → Export to download a JSON backup of all your data before deleting.

You can also disconnect individual integrations without deleting your account: Settings → Integrations removes the stored credentials for any service you no longer want connected.

  • Anthropic (USA) — SCCs.
  • Google (USA) — SCCs + EU-US DPF.
  • Microsoft (USA) — SCCs + EU-US DPF.
  • xAI, Meta, Cerebras (USA) — SCCs.
  • ElevenLabs (USA/UK) — SCCs.
  • Spotify (Sweden/EU) — no transfer issue.
  • Mapy.cz (Czech Republic/EU) — no transfer issue.
  • VUT / Moodle (Czech Republic/EU) — no transfer issue.
  • Tavily, Brave, DuckDuckGo, LRCLIB, ntfy.sh (USA) — SCCs.

You should review each provider's own privacy policy, as they act as independent controllers or processors of the data you send them. You can avoid non-EU transfers entirely by using a self-hosted Ollama LLM and not connecting US-based integrations.
